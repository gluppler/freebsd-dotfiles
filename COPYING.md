# Licenses and attribution

This repository contains configuration, original helpers, upstream patches,
fonts and artwork with different terms. Existing copyright and license notices
remain attached to their files. There is no blanket license for the entire tree.

- Each patched program keeps its upstream notice in its own folder, such as
  `foot/LICENSE`. dwl adds `LICENSE.dwm`, `LICENSE.sway`, `LICENSE.tinywl` and its
  IPC protocol's `IPC-PROTOCOL-LICENSE`; rustup has `LICENSE-APACHE` and
  `LICENSE-MIT`. Source versions and download locations are in
  `.install/sources.lock` and `.install/ports.lock`.
- Files explicitly marked ISC use the [ISC license](#isc-license) below. This
  does not change the license of upstream code included in a patch.
- Michroma and Azeret Mono retain their SIL Open Font License files under
  `fonts/`.
- The space_dots shader and its adaptations retain Maxim Samoliuk's MIT notice.
- Retained artwork and its sources are documented in `art/README.md` and
  `docs/SOURCES.md`. The Cyberpunk logo wallpapers and their attribution and
  usage notice are archived outside this repository.

Native builds copy their upstream notices into the installed license directory.
Research snapshots under `docs/research/` retain their original notices and are
not installed as programs.

Downloaded cppreference pages retain their attribution and CC BY-SA notices.
The downloaded reference tree is not included in the source export. Its exact
source and checksum are in `.install/sources.lock`.

The offline library (the `doc-*` records of `.install/sources.lock`) is downloaded during
installation and is not part of this repository or its export; each document
keeps its publisher's terms, summarized in `docs/OFFLINE-DOCS.md`. The chapters
in `docs/secret-knowledge/` are selected from The Book of Secret Knowledge by
trimstray and remain under its MIT license, copied in
`docs/secret-knowledge/LICENSE.md`.

The electronics guide in `docs/electronics/` is generated by `.install/electronics/`;
its figures and equations are drawn by that code, not copied. Its badge facts
come from the Tildagon documentation (CC BY 4.0), which the chapter credits. Its
equations use Noto Sans Math from the `noto-sans-math` package, under the SIL Open
Font License.
